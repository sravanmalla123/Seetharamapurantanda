Privacy Policy

Official guidelines concerning information protection, survey data security, and grievance encryption on the Seetharampuram Thanda Panchayat Portal.

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the Seetharampuram Thanda Gram Panchayat Digital Portal. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our residents. This policy outlines the kinds of information collected through our official portal, how we process it, and the data protection measures in place under current municipal and electronic communication frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

To provide direct administrative certificates, support agrarian planning, and audit public basic facilities, the portal processes three categories of data:

  • Survey Demographics: Anonymous, aggregated survey metrics including population figures, literacy rates, age group distributions, and student transport rolls.
  • Self-Reported Household Upgrades: Voluntary submissions from families updating their amenity status (such as clean energy adoption, toilet usage, and tap water connections) using household identifiers.
  • Public Grievances: Voluntary complaints and tickets submitted by residents containing their name, telephone contact, and details of municipal issues.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected details are strictly utilized to coordinate and improve local administrative operations, including:

  • Tracking and adjusting the Smart Village Rating (SVR) evaluations.
  • Directing Panchayat budget allocations to under-served areas (e.g. scheduling desilting works for southern drainage sectors, solar battery checks).
  • Forwarding public grievances to the relevant local authorities (AE Panchayat Engineering, Health Subcenter ASYN units) for direct resolution.

4. Data Security & Encryption

The security of resident data is a top priority. All submitted personal credentials, contact phone numbers, and grievance descriptions are secured with local **AES-256 encryption** algorithms. This prevents unauthorized database modifications and ensures that sensitive communication is accessible only to authorized administrative staff.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or distribute resident profiles or survey records to third-party organizations. Information is disclosed only to Mandal/District government engineering teams or utility technical units directly involved in resolving specific grievances or deploying infrastructure budgets.

6. Resident Rights

Residents maintain complete ownership of their voluntarily submitted data. You have the right to inspect your self-reported household profiles, request corrections of typographical errors, or request the deletion of active grievance logs by visiting the Panchayat Office.

7. Contact Information

For questions regarding data privacy rules or to request a record review, you may contact the Office of the Panchayat Secretary or Sarpanch Devika at the Gram Panchayat Headquarters, Seetharampuram Thanda, Mylavaram Mandal, NTR District, Andhra Pradesh.
