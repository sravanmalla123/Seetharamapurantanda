ABOUT THE VILLAGE & TRIBAL PROFILE

Seetharampuram Thanda History & Demographics

Explore the unique history, cultural heritage, geographical details, and comprehensive profile metrics of our Gram Panchayat.

Our Heritage & Settlement

History: Seetharampuram Thanda was settled several generations ago by families from the Banjara (Lambadi) tribe who migrated to the rocky terrains of Andhra Pradesh. Starting as a temporary settlement (Thanda), it grew into a permanent resilient agricultural hub.

Naming Origin: The name "Seetharampuram" is derived from the historic Seetharama Temple located on the outskirts of the hills, where the community continues to offer prayers. The suffix "Thanda" is the traditional term for a Banjara settlement.

Tribal Identity & Culture: The village is predominantly inhabited by the Lambadi community, preserving ancestral customs, traditional multi-colored embroidered dresses featuring mirrors and cowrie shells, and dancing to Teej festival folk songs.

Geography & Language: Nestled in the Palnadu district of Andhra Pradesh, our residents speak Gor Boli (the native Lambadi dialect) internally, and Telugu or Hindi for external trade. The nearby towns are Macherla (15 km) and Nagarjuna Sagar (28 km).

About Seetharampuram Thanda Gram Panchayat

Seetharampuram Thanda is a vibrant Gram Panchayat nestled in rural India. Home to a tight-knit community of generations, our village primarily relies on agricultural cultivations of Paddy, Maize, Chili, and Pulses.

Our administration is dedicated to blending civic sustainability (smart water recharge, tree-planting audits, digital notices) with the prideful preservation of our Lambadi/Banjara tribal culture, traditional eco-houses, and handicrafts.

Rule 1: Never Share Your OTP or PIN

Panchayat officials, bank officers, or government secretaries will NEVER call you on the phone asking for your One Time Password (OTP) or card PIN. Keep them private.

Rule 2: Check the Lock Icon in URL Bar

Before entering your username or password on any digital banking portal, ensure the site address begins with "https://" and has a padlock symbol beside it.
