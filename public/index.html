COMMON QUERIES

Citizen Helpdesk FAQ

Quick answers on civic documents, application wait times, and direct portal usage guidelines.

You need your 12-digit Survey Number, Aadhaar Card for identification, and the registered landowner name particulars. Applications can be submitted directly through the Vital Certificates tab.

Standard civic issues (like water valve leaks or garbage clearing) are inspected within 24–48 hours. Major road repairs or electrical grid concerns may take 7–10 working days. You can track your status live in the Grievance tab.

All adult residents of the Seetharamapuram Tanda Gram Panchayat holding a valid Job Card are eligible for standard daily manual labor. Wages are set at ₹300/day. You can browse active muster rolls in the Employment Portal.

Parents can apply by submitting a resident verification letter from the Ward Member and a study certificate from the high school. Once verified, free passes for the Gram Panchayat Shuttle are issued at the school complex.

Civic & Livelihood Welfare Schemes

Rythu Bandhu (Farmer Investment Support)

Agriculture

Provides seasonal input support grants of ₹5,000 per acre per season directly to farmers for buying seeds, fertilizer, and labor inputs.

Eligibility: Land-owning farming families in the village land register.

Aasara Pensions (Elderly & Widow Support)

Social Security

Provides monthly financial pensions of ₹2,016 to secure livelihoods for elderly villagers, widows, and local manual weavers.

Eligibility: Age over 57, widows, weaver artisans with family income under ₹1.5 Lakhs.

Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak

Community

One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 given during weddings to prevent wedding debts among minority families.

Eligibility: Brides aged 18+ from low-income families (income < ₹2 Lakhs).

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Central Scheme

Direct benefit transfer of ₹6,000 per year paid in three equal installments to rural landholder farmer families.

Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers holding cultivable land records.

About Seetharamapuram Tanda Gram Panchayat

Seetharamapuram Tanda is a vibrant Gram Panchayat nestled in rural India. Home to a tight-knit community of generations, our village primarily relies on agricultural cultivations of Paddy, Maize, Chili, and Pulses.

Our administration is dedicated to blending civic sustainability (smart water recharge, tree-planting audits, digital notices) with the prideful preservation of our Lambadi/Banjara tribal culture, traditional eco-houses, and handicrafts.
